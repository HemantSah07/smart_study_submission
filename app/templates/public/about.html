Our Mission & Vision

Helping Students Study Smarter Through Intelligence

Smart Study was built for one purpose: to make sure no student wastes time studying the wrong things or falls behind without knowing it.

The Problem We Solve

Most students study by intuition — spending time on subjects they feel comfortable with rather than those that actually need work. When exam time arrives, the gaps become visible, but it's often too late to fix them.

Our Solution

Smart Study uses assessment data, performance history, and study habits to identify academic risk early — then generates a personalised study plan that allocates more time exactly where it's needed.

Who It's Built For

Smart Study is designed for students from Class 8 to Master level who want to take control of their academic performance — especially those balancing multiple subjects with varying difficulty, attendance patterns, and exam schedules.

Why We Built It

Smart Study is a Final Year Project by Hemant Kumar Sah (NP069826). It started from a simple observation: students need a coach, not just a planner. A coach that tells them what to focus on, not just when to study.
